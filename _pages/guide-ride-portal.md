The [WheelDash Ride Portal]({{ site.data.product.urls.portal_login }}) is where Amazfit riders review their rides on a map with detailed statistics, and where the community shares public rides, segments and leaderboards. Anyone can [browse public rides]({{ site.data.product.urls.public_rides }}) without an account.

Garmin rides stay in Garmin Connect; they are not uploaded to the Ride Portal.
{: .notice--info}

## Upload a ride

You need a WheelDash account, logged in in the Zepp app. See [install and first ride](/install_amazfit/#settings).

1. On the watch, open WheelDash and choose **Data Transfer** in the main menu. Keep the watch on this page during the upload.
2. In the Zepp app, open WheelDash's settings, then **Ride Manager**.
3. Tap **Upload** for a ride, or **Upload All**.

Keep the phone near the watch until the upload is complete.

## Review your rides

Sign in to the Ride Portal with the same email and password. **Rides** lists your recordings: open one to see it on the map with its speed, battery, temperature and other data, or select two to compare them side by side.

## Private and public rides

Your rides are private by default: only you can see them. They are encrypted on your phone before upload, with a key derived from your password, so the portal asks for your password to open them.

To share a ride, open it and choose **Make Public**, with an optional description. Public rides appear in Discover, can count towards the leaderboards and help build community segments. You can make a ride private again at any time. Your account settings also let you choose the default visibility.

**Privacy zones** hide places such as your home or work from the maps of your public rides. Create them from a ride's map; they are applied before anything is published.

## Community

- **[Discover]({{ site.data.product.urls.public_rides }}):** search public rides, riders, wheels and places.
- **[Leaderboards]({{ site.data.product.urls.leaderboard }}):** speed, distance, contributions and streaks, from public rides only.
- **[Segments]({{ site.data.product.urls.segments }}):** compare wheels over the same public road sections. Segments grow as riders share rides over the same roads, so some areas have little activity yet.

The Ride Portal also hosts the [dashboard layout editor](/guides/amazfit-dashboards/) and the [route upload for navigation](/guides/amazfit-navigation/).
