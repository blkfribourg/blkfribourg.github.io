<section class="vesc-capabilities">
  <div><p class="vesc-kicker">What it does</p><h2>Controller data without the clutter.</h2><p>VESCDash is focused on the information VESC riders need while moving and the ride record they want afterward.</p></div>
  <div class="vesc-capabilities__grid">
    <article><h3>Live telemetry</h3><p>Display speed, battery, PWM, voltage, current, motor power, and controller temperature.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Garmin activities</h3><p>Record routes and VESC metrics as activities in the Garmin ecosystem.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Direct BLE connection</h3><p>Connect supported Garmin watches to recognized VESC controllers over Bluetooth.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Independent application</h3><p>VESCDash has its own releases and audience while sharing some foundations with WheelDash.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
