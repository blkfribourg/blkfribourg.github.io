## Ready-made dashboards

In the WheelDash settings, **User Interface Layout** offers four choices:

- **Default:** a PWM ring, short battery and temperature arcs, the clock on top and the speed in the centre.
- **Large:** bigger figures, for riders who need them, with a thin PWM ring.
- **Racing:** four large values, no clock and no PWM bar.
- **Custom:** the layout you build yourself.

All of them keep the same zones: the clock or the alarm banner on top, the figures, the Varia band (only while the radar tracks a vehicle), and a row showing recording and the speed limiter.

## Build your own

A custom dashboard is made of blocks you place and size freely: clock, plain text, circular gauges, values, labelled values, progress bars, separators, the Varia radar bar, the recording indicator, the speed-limit indicator and the alarm message. Values, gauges and bars can show any wheel or ride value, from speed and PWM to tyre pressure when the wheel reports it.

You can build it in two places:

- **In the Zepp app:** WheelDash settings, **Custom Dashboard**.
- **On the Ride Portal:** the [layout editor](https://wd.nijalabs.com/layout-editor) gives you a larger screen. Use **Save to Cloud**, then **Load from cloud** in the Zepp app's **Custom Dashboard** to bring the layout to your watch.

Select the shape of your watch before you start, round or square. Layout changes apply the next time WheelDash starts.

## Use someone else's layout

The Ride Portal's layout gallery shows dashboards shared by other riders. Open one in the editor, or save it to your cloud and load it in the Zepp app.

Then set **User Interface Layout** to **Custom** to use it.
