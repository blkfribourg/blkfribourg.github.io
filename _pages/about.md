WheelDash is an independently developed project built by an electric unicycle rider. It puts useful EUC information, warnings and ride tools on compatible Garmin and Amazfit watches.

## Why WheelDash exists

WheelDash began as a personal solution to a simple riding problem: a phone is an inconvenient place for information that needs to remain visible while moving.

The first Garmin version connected directly to the EUC, displayed live telemetry, managed safety alarms and recorded rides. Feedback from other riders helped turn that personal project into a broader set of tools for the EUC community.

## WheelDash today

WheelDash now exists as a Garmin App, a Garmin Datafield and an Amazfit app, with the WheelDash Ride Portal for reviewing and sharing Amazfit rides. In direct EUC mode, the watch connects straight to the wheel: it does not need WheelLog, DarknessBot or EUC World as a live-data middleman.

What each version can do, and which watches and EUCs it supports, is on the [platform comparison](/platforms/).

## Open source and funding

The Garmin versions and VESCDash are free and open source. The Amazfit version is also free, and its source code will be released once its active development phase is complete.

WheelDash has no advertising. Donations help cover development time and the servers behind the Ride Portal and navigation; read about [donations and future funding](/donate/).

## Development and community

Development is informed by real riding needs and community feedback, and platform limitations are documented rather than hidden.
