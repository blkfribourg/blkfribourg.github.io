VESCDash is currently available only for Garmin and is distributed through Garmin Connect IQ. Compatibility is determined by the Garmin device selected in the store.

## Choose a release

### Stable release

Recommended for everyday use. Stable releases prioritize tested behavior and compatibility.

### Development release

Development releases provide newer changes for testing and may be less stable, particularly for controller or watch combinations the developer cannot test directly.

## Before installing

- Confirm that your Garmin watch supports Bluetooth Low Energy and Connect IQ 3.1 or later.
- VESCDash is designed for most VESC controllers running firmware 6.2 or later.
- Some controller Bluetooth advertising names must be recognized explicitly. [Contact the developer](/contact/) if your controller is not detected.

[Read the VESCDash documentation](/vescdash/docs/) before your first connection.
